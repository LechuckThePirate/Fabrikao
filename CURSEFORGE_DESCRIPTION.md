# Fabrikao!!

**Your professions companion: browse recipes, plan what to craft, and find out where to get every recipe and ingredient.**

For WoW Forever. Open it with `/fab`, the minimap button or a key binding.

## What it does

- **Your professions:** a window with your professions (the primary ones first, then First Aid, Cooking and Fishing), each with its skill bar.
- **Every recipe:** each profession lists the recipes you know, colored by difficulty as in the game, with how many you can make from your bags in brackets, and then the ones you don't know yet, with the skill they need and where they are learned. Fold or unfold each group with a click.
- **Table or detailed view:** columns with the ingredients as icons (with their tooltips), the approximate cost, the auction value and the level, or a big icon with three lines per recipe. Choose it in the preferences.
- **Filters and sorting:** by where it is learned (trainer, vendor, quest, drop...), by category (hands, wrist, bags, potions, elixirs...), by color for your skill, "learnable now" or "needs more skill", "what I can make now", hide grey recipes, and sorting by name, level, cost, auction value or how many you can make.
- **Search every recipe in the game:** by name, ingredient, vendor, creature, quest or zone, also for professions you don't have (the box above your professions, or `/fab find <text>`), with the same filters.
- **A panel for each recipe:** click a recipe to open everything known about it: your skill and its colors, what it makes, the ingredients with how many you have (bags, bank and other characters), what they cost, what it sells for and the profit, the training cost, the item that teaches it and every trainer, vendor, quest and creature that gives it, nearest first, only the ones of your faction.
- **Find them on the map:** each trainer, vendor and creature has a "Map" button that opens the world map on it, and a "TomTom" button (with TomTom installed) that sets a waypoint.

## Works with

- **Embolsao:** count the bags and banks of your other characters in what you can make.
- **Auctionator:** auction prices for the cost and the value (without it, what vendors pay).
- **TomTom:** waypoints to trainers and vendors.

None of them is needed: without them everything else works.

## Commands

| Command | What it does |
|---|---|
| `/fabrikao` or `/fab` | Opens or closes the window |
| `/fab find <text>` | Searches every recipe |
| `/fab minimap` | Shows or hides the minimap button |
| `/fab version` | The addon's version |
